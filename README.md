# Numismatic Overhaul — Earn Coins and Build Your Shop Empire

Numismatic Overhaul 9minecraft Mod replaces the classic emerald economy with a three-tier coin currency. You earn bronze, silver, and gold coins from slaying monsters, exploring loot chests, and trading with villagers. A personal purse system keeps your coins organized so your inventory never fills up with loose change. You can also build custom shops to trade goods with other players on multiplayer worlds.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Noaaan**, **glisco**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
